1. Overview

The handbook is recorded as an HR Handbook record. The system applies its attendance, tardiness, half-day, permission and leave rules automatically from the fingerprint check-ins sent by the ZKTeco devices. Where a policy contradicts the Egyptian Labour Law, the law prevails (handbook disclaimer).

  1. Check-in: each punch arrives as an Employee Checkin.
  2. Attendance: HRMS turns the day's check-ins into an Attendance (Present, Absent, Half Day, On Leave).
  3. Handbook rules: the handbook adjusts that record (for example a Half Day for arriving after 11:00) and writes the reason in Policy Classification.
  4. Nightly processing: penalties, half-day deductions, leave allocations and overtime records are created.
  5. Review: HR approves or dismisses each penalty and deduction before it takes effect.
  6. Dashboard: each employee sees their month at /hr-dashboard.

4. Tardiness (§8)

Lateness is added up over the calendar month. From 45 counted minutes a salary deduction is proposed; HR approves it.

  1. Late minutes = first check-in − 09:00.
  2. Approved permission time is removed (§8.2.5).
  3. Grace: 10 minutes or less is forgiven; 11 or more counts in full.
  4. Forgiven minutes are capped at 40 per month (§8.1.2); above that they count as late.

At the start of each month the system creates one draft Attendance Penalty per employee who reached a tier, listing every late day. Submit to apply, Cancel to dismiss (never recreated), Amend to correct. The penalty value is the number of days' salary.

5. Half day (§8.1.1)

Arriving after 11:00 or leaving before 16:00 without prior approval makes the day a Half Day, and 0.5 day is deducted from Annual Leave after HR approval.

  • First check-in after 11:00 → Half Day, unless an approved Late Arrival permission covers the whole delay.
  • Last check-out before 16:00 → Half Day, unless an approved Early Departure permission covers it.

The next night a draft Half Day Leave Deduction (0.5 Annual Leave) is created. Submitting deducts; cancelling restores. If the balance is too low it stays a draft for HR.

6. Permissions (§4.6.5 / §8.2.5)

Once a month, up to 2 hours, with the manager's prior consent: recorded as an Employee Permission.

  1. Employee or HR: Employee Permission › New; choose Late Arrival, Early Departure or Temporary Absence.
  2. Set date, start and end time, reason. Repetition stays One-time.
  3. Manager or HR sets Approval Status to Approved and submits. Employees cannot approve their own.

Limits: 1 permission and 120 minutes per month; overlapping permissions are refused. Approved permission time is never late time and prevents a Half Day.

Example: Late Arrival 09:00–11:00 on 12 Nov, approved; arrives 10:50 → not late, no half day. A second request on 20 Nov is refused: Only 1 permission(s) are allowed in the period 2026-11-01 – 2026-11-30.

§4.6.5.1 (extra requests → half-day deduction): the system refuses them instead; HR records any exception manually.

10. Employee dashboard

Every employee sees their month at /hr-dashboard; only their own data. HR can enter an Employee ID.

  • Cards: working, worked and absent days; late minutes; deductions; overtime (approved and pending); leave taken; annual leave left; half days; WFH days.
  • Daily table: date, shift, first in, last out, hours, late minutes, status, notes (holidays, leave, permissions, missing punches).
  • Below: leave balances, deductions, overtime.

Assumptions: the 40-minute cap applies to forgiven minutes; accumulated tardiness is counted after grace; "half day leave" means 0.5 Annual Leave; first-year leave accrues 1.75 per month and is usable after 6 months; probation ends 6 months after joining unless a confirmation date is set.

Legal points to confirm (the Labour Law prevails):

  • Annual leave: Law 14/2025 gives 15 days in the first year, 21 from the second, 30 after 10 years or from age 50 (age rule not automated).
  • Overtime pay: at least +35% day / +70% night per published reports; another reading gives +25% day. Tracked at 1.0 until confirmed.
  • Weekly rest day work: day's pay plus a day off; public holidays: double pay or a compensatory day.
  • Maternity leave length and the two-times limit.
  • Legal limits on tardiness salary deductions.
